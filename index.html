Common questions

What is CreditCalcFin?

It helps you estimate your credit health from information you enter, review score factors, try scenarios, track accounts, and plan debt payments. Its estimates are educational, not official credit bureau scores or lending decisions.

How do I start or repeat an assessment?

Follow the guided assessment when you first open the app. Later, open the Tracker tab to review your assessment history or tap Reassess Credit Health to start again.

How do daily points work?

Open Profile → Daily Check-In to earn 10 points once per day. You can use 10 points to unlock a debt plan's full monthly details or a comparison report for two saved scenarios. Points are stored on this device.

How do I manage saved information?

Open Profile → Privacy & Data to export or delete locally saved assessments, accounts, scenarios, goals, reminders, rewards, and profile details. Deleting local data cannot be undone.
